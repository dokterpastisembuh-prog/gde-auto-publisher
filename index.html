GDE Auto Publisher

by Good Doctors

GDE Auto Publisher is an internal tool used by Good Doctors to publish our own educational health videos to our own social media accounts, including TikTok, on a schedule.

How it works

  1. Our team produces short educational health videos.
  2. Each video is reviewed and approved by our team before publishing.
  3. The approved video is uploaded to our TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API, after the account owner has authorized the app with TikTok Login.

The tool is not offered to the public and only posts to accounts owned by Good Doctors.
