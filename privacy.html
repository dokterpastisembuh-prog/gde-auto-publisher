Privacy Policy

GDE Auto Publisher · Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. Overview

GDE Auto Publisher ("the Service") is an internal tool operated by Good Doctors that publishes our own videos to our own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the Service handles.

2. Data we receive from TikTok

When the account owner authorizes the Service with TikTok Login, we receive:

  • An access token and refresh token, used only to publish videos to the authorized account;
  • Basic account information (such as display name and avatar) and the account's available privacy settings, used only to confirm which account is connected and to choose a valid privacy level for each post;
  • The publishing status and post ID of videos we upload.

3. How we use the data

The data is used solely to upload and publish videos created by Good Doctors and to check whether publishing succeeded. We do not sell, rent, or share this data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored encrypted in our automation platform (n8n) and are accessible only to authorized Good Doctors staff. Publishing logs (post IDs and status) are kept for operational records.

5. Data of other TikTok users

The Service does not collect data about viewers, followers, or other TikTok users.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the connection is active. The account owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Security > Manage app permissions); we then delete the stored tokens. Deletion requests can also be sent to the contact below.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
